# Kučišdorf and Pezinská Baba loop

Planning assumptions: central Bratislava start, dry daylight conditions, 3–4 hours available, paved motorcycle routing, and short walks accepted.

## Shape

1. Bratislava centre
2. Stĺp hanby, Bernolákovo — quick roadside oddity
3. Vahadlová studňa approach — park and inspect the walk; skip if access looks poor
4. Kučišdorfská priehrada — short waterside stop from mapped parking
5. Miesto poranenia Ľudovíta Štúra — park and walk; do not ride the restricted service way
6. Pezinská Baba, II/503 — the riding payoff, east to west
7. Return to Bratislava through Pernek and Lozorno

## Scale

- OSRM baseline: approximately 110 km and 2 hours 10 minutes of driving.
- Practical total: approximately 3 hours 30 minutes to 4 hours with the two walks and short stops.
- The route estimate is a baseline, not a motorcycle-aware route. Import the GPX into Kurviger and let it choose the final connecting roads.

## Access discipline

- The well, reservoir, and memorial target coordinates are not used blindly as routing points.
- GPX uses mapped parking/approach coordinates for those targets.
- Vahadlová studňa remains `needs_research`; treat the visit as reconnaissance.
- The Štúr memorial is explicitly park-and-walk because a nearby service way is mapped `motor_vehicle=no`.
